What is Display?

Display is a new feature in Slabfolio, the card tracking app I'm building. It turns your collection into a page you can share with one link.

  • 🃏Pick your cards. Split them into For sale, PC and Sold.
  • 👁️Choose what shows. Prices, descriptions and grading notes are yours to switch on or off. Tap Edit to try it.
  • 💬Buyers message you. Buy, make an offer, or bundle a few cards into one message.
  • 🔗Share any card. Every card has its own link.
